## 학술 논문 / 기관 공식 자료

1. IMF (2019), *Global Financial Stability Report*, Chapter 2 — *Downside Risks to House Prices*. https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/gfsr/2019/april/english/ch2.pdf
2. Adrian, T., Deghi, A., Katagiri, M., Shahid, S., Valckx, N. (2020), *Predicting Downside Risks to House Prices and Macro-Financial Stability*, IMF Working Paper WP/20/11. https://www.imf.org/en/-/media/files/publications/wp/2020/english/wpiea2020011-print-pdf.pdf
3. Phillips, P. C. B., Shi, S., Yu, J. (2015), *Testing for Multiple Bubbles: Historical Episodes of Exuberance and Collapse in the S&P 500*, *International Economic Review* 56(4), 1043–1078, https://doi.org/10.1111/iere.12132
4. OECD, *Housing prices* (analytical house price indicators). https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/housing-prices.html
5. BIS, *Residential property prices — BIS Data Portal*. https://data.bis.org/topics/RPP

## 적용상의 유의사항

OECD/BIS 국가단위 백분위는 지역별 하락확률이 아니며, 임의의 5% 위험확률 같은 수치를 국내 모형 검증 없이 표시하지 않습니다. 논문·이론 링크는 **근거 읽기용**이고 검증 PASS의 증거로 간주하지 않습니다.
